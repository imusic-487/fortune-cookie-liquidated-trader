One fortune cookie line for a crypto trader who just got liquidated,
written for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-clever-harbor-75-fortune-cookie-for-the-liquidated-38fd239c).

## The fortune

> Fortune favors the liquidated trader who laughs at 3 a.m.: your wallet is empty, your story is finally rich.

19 words, one line, plain text. It keeps the oracular fortune voice,
names the trader and the loss outright, stays rueful and warm instead of
cruel, and lands its turn on the last word — "rich" flips from money,
which is gone, to the story, which is finally worth telling.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the fortune to `/output/entry.txt` — nothing else, no
network, no external files.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
